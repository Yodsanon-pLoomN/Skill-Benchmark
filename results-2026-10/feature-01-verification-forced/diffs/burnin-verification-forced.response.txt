**What I added:**
- `Controllers/BookingsController.cs` handles `PATCH /bookings/{id}/extend` with body `{ "newEndsAt": ... }`.
- `Services/BookingExtensionService.cs` applies the four rules.
- `Program.cs` registers the new service.

**What each rule returns:**
- **Unknown booking id:** 404.
- **Missing `newEndsAt`:** 400.
- **Rule 1, booking is not `CONFIRMED`:** 409.
- **Rule 2, `newEndsAt` is not strictly after the current `EndsAt`:** 400.
- **Rule 3, total duration from the original `StartsAt` is over 8 hours:** 400. Exactly 8 hours is allowed.
- **Rule 4, overlap with another `CONFIRMED` booking in the same room:** 409. It uses `a.StartsAt < b.EndsAt && a.EndsAt > b.StartsAt`, so a booking that ends exactly when another starts is allowed. The booking itself and cancelled bookings are excluded.
- **Success:** it saves the new `EndsAt` and returns the updated booking with 200.

I picked those status codes myself; the spec only said "4xx".

`newEndsAt` is parsed as a timestamp with an offset and converted to UTC before comparing, because the seed data is stored as UTC.
