**New file:** `Controllers/BookingsController.cs` — `PATCH /bookings/{id}/extend`, body `{ "newEndsAt": "..." }`.

Checks, in order:
- Missing `newEndsAt`: 400.
- Booking not found: 404.
- Status not `CONFIRMED`: 409.
- `newEndsAt <= EndsAt`: 400.
- `newEndsAt - StartsAt > 8h`: 400.
- Overlap with another `CONFIRMED` booking in same room (`a.Start < b.End && a.End > b.Start`, half-open, own booking excluded): 409.
- Success: saves `EndsAt`, returns 200 with the booking.

Design choices:
- Overlap compare runs in memory, not SQL, because SQLite stores `DateTime` as text.
- All times treated as UTC. That matches the seed data. A `newEndsAt` with an offset is converted to UTC.
